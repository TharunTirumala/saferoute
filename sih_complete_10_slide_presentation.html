1. Problem Statement
SIH Evaluation Point 1

The Flaw in Standard Navigation

Current mapping applications (Google Maps, Waze) prioritize travel time and shortest distance above all else.

⚠️ Problem: Standard algorithms frequently guide female pedestrians and solo travelers through unlit back-alleys or high-crime zones just to save 60 seconds.
  • Lack of Safety Aware Routing: No evaluation of street lighting, crime hazards, or police proximity.
  • Delayed Emergency Response: Traditional SOS apps fail on mobile due to CORS restrictions or latency.
  • Zero Explainability: Users are not told WHY a route is unsafe.

Evidence & Quantitative Impact

[Standard Maps Goal] Distance / Time Optimization Only │ ▼ (Routes through unlit alley) ❌ Increased Exposure to Crime Hazards ❌ Zero Police Proximity Scoring ❌ No Lighting Infrastructure Tags
Key Need: An intelligent navigation system that prioritizes Safety First over travel distance.
